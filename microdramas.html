MICRODRAMA

What if Instagram Reels looked like they belonged on the big screen?

This series is my love letter to the internet's obsession with "Instagram Prime," where every frame is cinematic enough to make you stop scrolling. Each episode was shot on richly textured sets with intentional lighting, thoughtful framing, and way too much time spent making every little detail look just right. The aim was to explore how vertical video can be immersive, polished, and visually ambitious in its own right, standing alongside traditional filmmaking.
