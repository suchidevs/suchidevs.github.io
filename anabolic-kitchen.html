ANABOLIC KITCHEN

People connect with people. That's why, instead of marketing Anabolic Kitchen like a typical fitness brand, I built a founder-led content strategy that made Killi, the owner, the face of it all.

Taking inspiration from food creators and personality-first content, I leaned into authentic storytelling over polished advertising. The goal was simple: create content that felt less like a brand talking at you and more like spending time with someone you'd actually want to watch.

That shift created a flywheel. Community engagement grew rapidly, high-profile brand partnerships started reaching out directly to Killi, and Anabolic Kitchen became a living case study.
